Getting from Hanoi to Ha Giang

By Matthew Lee · Last updated: August 2026

A sleeper bus, usually overnight, taking around six to seven hours. It costs roughly 350,000 VND (about $13–14) per person, and I book it for guests at cost — I do not add a margin to it.

The short answer

Sleeper bus, around six to seven hours, usually overnight. You arrive early in the morning, sleep and shower, and the trip starts after breakfast rather than straight off the bus.

Which bus companies

Dang Quang (Đăng Quang), Quang Tuyen (Quang Tuyến) and Bang Phan (Bằng Phấn) are the operators running this route. Expect to pay around 350,000 VND (roughly $13–14) per person.

What to expect on board

These are proper sleeper buses — flat-ish reclined berths, not upright seats — built for exactly this route. Bring a layer; air conditioning on overnight buses in Vietnam runs cold. Keep valuables and your passport with you rather than in checked luggage underneath.

Booking it yourself, or through me

You can book directly with any of the three operators above. If you are travelling with me, I book it at cost as part of arranging your trip — I do not add a margin on the ticket.

Where this is wrong for you

If you cannot sleep on moving vehicles, six to seven hours on a night bus is a genuinely rough start to the trip — a daytime option or an extra rest day in Ha Giang City before starting may suit you better. Tell me and I will plan around it rather than assume you'll be fine.

Arriving in Ha Giang City

Buses arrive early morning. On my trips you sleep, shower and eat breakfast before we start — the loop does not begin the moment you step off the bus. Once you're here, the Ha Giang Loop guide covers everything that comes next.
