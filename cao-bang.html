The experience

Sit back on a comfortable 150cc motorbike with a backrest while your rider takes the handlebars — one guest, one rider, so every stop is your stop.

We climb the legendary passes of Ha Giang, drift along the emerald Nho Que River, then keep going into the quiet, wild border country of Cao Bang: past Lo Lo and Tay villages, over the 14-turn Me Pia Pass, all the way to Ban Gioc Waterfall — and we sleep right next to it, so you see it at sunrise before anyone else arrives.

Meals, rooms, water and even your limousine bus ticket back to Hanoi are all taken care of.

The route

Day by day

Day 1

Ha Giang → Dong Van

±150 km · riding with plenty of stops

  1. 8:30 AM

    Pick up at your hotel in Ha Giang. We climb Bac Sum Pass to Quan Ba Heaven Gate for the famous Twin Mountains view.

  2. Midday

    Lunch in Yen Minh town, then the H'Mong King's Palace and the winding Tham Ma Pass.

  3. Evening

    Night 1 — Dong Van old town. Private room, hot shower, comfortable bed.

Day 2

Dong Van → Lung Cu → Meo Vac

±90 km · a shorter, relaxed day

  1. Morning

    Lung Cu Flag Tower — the northernmost point of Vietnam.

  2. Highlight

    Ma Pi Leng Pass and the emerald Nho Que River far below. We take our time — coffee with a view included. Optional boat trip through Tu San Canyon.

  3. Evening

    Night 2 — Meo Vac. Private room.

Day 3

Meo Vac → Bao Lac

±130 km · quiet, wild, no crowds

  1. All day

    We leave the crowds behind. This quiet stretch into Cao Bang province sees few tourists. We pass through villages of the Lo Lo and Tay people.

  2. Evening

    Night 3 — Bao Lac. Private room in a family-run guesthouse; the home cooking is excellent.

Day 4

Bao Lac → Me Pia Pass → Ban Gioc

±160 km through limestone valleys

  1. Morning

    Viewpoint over the famous 14-turn Me Pia Pass, then on through Cao Bang's limestone valleys.

  2. Afternoon

    Arrive at Ban Gioc Waterfall — one of the largest waterfalls in Southeast Asia, right on the China border.

  3. Evening

    Night 4 — sleeping near the falls, so the morning is yours before the buses arrive.

Day 5

Ban Gioc → Nguom Ngao Cave → Cao Bang

±90 km · relaxed all-day pace

  1. Sunrise

    Sunrise at Ban Gioc Waterfall — just you and the sound of the water.

  2. Morning

    Explore Nguom Ngao Cave, then a relaxed ride back toward Cao Bang city with stops along the way.

  3. Afternoon

    Your rider helps you board an afternoon limousine bus to Hanoi — ticket included, comfortable reclining seats, arriving around 10:00 PM.

Is this the right trip for me?

No licence needed and nothing to ride yourself — you sit behind an experienced local rider for the whole route. This is the longest trip on offer and the only one that leaves Ha Giang province.

When to come

  • Sep–Nov and Mar–May — The best months. Clear light, dry roads, comfortable temperatures.
  • Dec–Feb — Spectacular, and genuinely cold at altitude. Bring more layers than you think.
  • Jun–Aug — Green and wet, with the waterfalls at their best — and the months when landslides happen. I will tell you honestly if your dates are a bad window.
