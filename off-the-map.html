The experience

Most Ha Giang tours are the same tour: thirty bikes in a line, six photo stops, a bus back to Hanoi. This one starts somewhere else.

Day one never touches the main Loop. We ride dirt tracks west to a cave and to Khau Lan — a waterfall with no ticket booth and usually nobody in it. Lunch is on the floor of a Dao family home. Sunset is from a H'mong villa.

Then the famous road, properly: Lung Cu on the Chinese border, Dong Van old quarter, silver hammered by hand in Lao Xa, hemp stripped and indigo-dyed the way it always has been. A kayak on green water. A night on the White Cliff, if you want it.

The route

Day by day

Day 1 · Off-road and trekking

Ha Giang City → Khau Lan → H'mong villa

3–4 hours on foot · the hardest day, taken while your legs are fresh

  1. Off the tarmac by mid-morning

    Night bus in, shower, eat, meet your bike. Then west on dirt tracks the Loop never turns onto.

  2. A cave, then the trek to Khau Lan

    Leave the bikes and walk down through forest to a wild waterfall — cold, deep, swimmable. No gate, no crowd.

  3. Lunch with a Dao family

    Cooked over fire in their kitchen, eaten on the floor with them. Whatever the hillside gave that morning.

  4. Sunset at the villa

    Ride out to a H'mong villa for the light, then dinner and the night there.

Day 2 · The border and the craft villages

→ Lung Cu → Dong Van

A full riding day · night in Dong Van

  1. North to the border

    Can Ty pass in early light, the Tham Ma switchbacks, then the flag tower at Lung Cu, looking into China.

  2. Hemp and silver, by hand

    An hour with a family stripping, pounding and indigo-dyeing hemp. Then Lao Xa, where silver is still hammered in a courtyard. You try both.

  3. Dong Van old quarter

    Hundred-year-old shophouses, a coffee, dinner wherever smells best. Our own homestay — or upgrade if you'd rather.

Day 3 · The big one

Dong Van → Ma Pi Leng → Du Gia

Kayak · camp on the White Cliff, or a homestay in Du Gia

  1. Ma Pi Leng, slowly

    The pass everyone comes for — ridden with the stops your guide knows, not the ones on the map.

  2. Kayak on the green water

    Between limestone walls, five hundred metres below the road. No engine, no queue.

  3. Your choice for the night

    Standard: a family homestay in Du Gia. Or sleep in a tent on the White Cliff itself — our camp, gear carried up, dinner cooked on the ledge.

Day 4 · The quiet road home

Du Gia → Thuan Hoa → Thon Tha

2–3 hours on foot · tour ends 7:00pm · night bus 9:00pm

  1. The Thuan Hoa road

    Back the long, empty way. Terraces, small hamlets, almost no traffic — the best last ride in the province.

  2. Thon Tha, on foot

    A Tay village minutes from the city that every Loop tour drives past on the way to the bus station. Stilt houses, water wheels, rice terraces.

  3. Waterfall No. 6

    One last swim, and a proper meal to finish.

  4. 7:00pm — the end

    Hot shower, somewhere to repack. We drive you to the night bus or call your car.

Is this the right trip for me?

Day 1 is a real hill day on forest trails. If you walk or hike now and then, you'll love it. You never carry more than a day pack. Age 14 and over.

When to come

  • Sep–Nov and Mar–May — The best months. Clear light, dry roads, comfortable temperatures.
  • Dec–Feb — Spectacular, and genuinely cold at altitude. Bring more layers than you think.
  • Jun–Aug — Green and wet, with the waterfalls at their best — and the months when landslides happen. I will tell you honestly if your dates are a bad window.
