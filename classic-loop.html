The experience

Every big name on the Ha Giang Loop — Tham Ma, Lung Cu, Ma Pi Leng, Nho Que — plus the homestay tables and quiet valleys the convoys never stop at.

Day one climbs from Ha Giang City to the stone plateau. Day two is the one people come for: Ma Pi Leng ridden slowly, then down to the green water. Day three belongs to the water and the crafts.

Maximum five guests. No convoy. I run every trip myself.

The route

Day by day

Day 1 · Passes and the border

Ha Giang City → Lung Cu → Dong Van

A full riding day · night in a Dong Van homestay

  1. Coffee, then the keys

    Breakfast, bulky bags into the locker, pick your helmet, route briefing — and we ride.

  2. Thuan Hoa, Can Ty, Tham Ma

    The Boong waterfall viewpoint, Can Ty pass, Lao Va Chai, then the switchbacks everyone photographs. Lunch in Yen Minh.

  3. The palace, the village, the flag

    The H'mong King's Palace, Lo Lo Chai at the foot of the hill, and the tower at Lung Cu — the northernmost point of Vietnam.

  4. Dong Van after dark

    Dinner with our homestay family, then the old quarter — a fire, music, and shophouses a hundred years old.

Day 2 · The big one

Dong Van → Ma Pi Leng → Du Gia

Boat on the Nho Que · night in Du Gia village

  1. Ma Pi Leng, slowly

    One of Vietnam's four great passes — ridden with the stops your guide knows, not the ones on the map.

  2. Down to the green water

    A boat on the Nho Que, between limestone walls five hundred metres below the road. Then Meo Vac town.

  3. The valley road to Du Gia

    Lunch at Mau Due, then Tham Luong waterfall and down into Du Gia — the village the Loop rushes through.

  4. A night that runs long

    Family dinner at the homestay, rice wine if you want it, and whoever is still up when the music starts.

Day 3 · Water, craft and the road home

Du Gia → Lung Tam → Ha Giang City

Tour ends 7:00pm · night bus 9:00pm

  1. Morning under the waterfall

    Breakfast at the homestay, a swim at Tham Luong, and a walk through Du Gia before the heat.

  2. Lunch on the river

    Over the Duong Thuong road to the Ban An wharf, onto a boat, and lunch eaten on the water.

  3. Indigo, by hand

    Lung Tam weaving village — linen stripped, spun and dyed the old way. Then Thai An waterfall for the last swim.

  4. 7:00pm — the end

    Back the empty Thuan Hoa road. Hot shower, tea, somewhere to repack. We drive you to the night bus or call your car.

Is this the right trip for me?

This is the Loop done well — riding days, short walks, two swims. No trekking day, no gear to carry. If you want a full off-road day and a hike to a hidden waterfall, take Off The Map instead. Age 14 and over.

When to come

  • Sep–Nov and Mar–May — The best months. Clear light, dry roads, comfortable temperatures.
  • Dec–Feb — Spectacular, and genuinely cold at altitude. Bring more layers than you think.
  • Jun–Aug — Green and wet, with the waterfalls at their best — and the months when landslides happen. I will tell you honestly if your dates are a bad window.
